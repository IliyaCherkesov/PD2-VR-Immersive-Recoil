Simple mod to remove vanila recoil in VR while changing it with much cooler in-game model recoil that resembles PC version recoil. Made with assistance of GPT6. 

Description: this mod removes fully vanila recoil and adds logical recoil to weapons. It takes PC version recoil system of recoil and brings it to game. There is a difference between one hand hold of weapon, and when you
hold it with both hands - one hand setup punishes you with higher recoil than PC version, and two - hand stance gives you less recoil than PC version (in both scenarios you are punished by being obligated to hold controllers).
I made it as balanced as I could. If you find something being unfair/buggy - feel free to open issue. You will be provided with logger if problem is deeper than balance and we can solve it together.
